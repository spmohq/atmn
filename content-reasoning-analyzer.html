CONTENT REASONING ANALYZER

Analysis Logic Prompt

CONTEXT

Use the following reasoning hierarchy throughout the analysis.

- "Idea" → The author's expressed thought, viewpoint, or mental model.
- "Concept" → The underlying fundamental idea or reality that gives rise to the Idea.
- "Principle" → A general truth or universal principle derived from the Concept.
- "Rule" → A practical guideline or decision rule derived from the Principle.
- "Action" → What should be done according to the Rule.
- "Result" → The immediate outcome produced by taking the Action.
- "Transformation" → The long-term change produced by consistently achieving the right Results.

Important

- Every "Idea" or "Concept" is not necessarily true.
- An Idea or Concept is valid only when it is consistent with reality and its underlying Principles.
- If an Idea is based on a false Principle (an illusion accepted as truth), identify it as such.

BENGALI LOCALIZATION LAYER

Please write the output of the PROMPT below in the most spontaneous, natural-sounding modern conversational Bengali as spoken in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

The writing should be easy for a 21-year-old to understand while maintaining high editorial quality, clarity, precision, and natural readability.

Strict Rules

- Reflect Kolkata conversational cadence through sentence rhythm and phrasing by using slightly formal, polite, and smoothly flowing sentence structures with balanced pacing. Avoid slang, regional grammar, overly literary wording, or awkward literal translations.
- Always use the formal "আপনি", never "তুমি".
- The tone should remain warm, conversational, approachable, confident, and quietly authoritative.
- Write as if speaking directly to one person in a natural face-to-face conversation.
- Do not translate literally. Rewrite naturally so the output feels as though it was originally written in Bengali.
- Preserve the original meaning, intent, emotional tone, and persuasive effect without adding, removing, or changing information.

INPUT

The uploaded document.

---

TASK

Analyse the document and identify the following.

1. Idea

"Question:" What is the central Idea or mental model expressed by the author?

2. Concept

"Question:" What fundamental Concept or underlying reality gives rise to that Idea?

3. Principle

"Question:" What universal Principle naturally follows from that Concept?

4. Rule

"Question:" Based on that Principle, what Rule or decision guideline should be followed?

5. Action

"Question:" According to that Rule, what Action should be taken?

6. Result

"Question:" What immediate Result is expected from taking that Action?

7. Transformation

"Question:" If those Results are achieved consistently over time, what long-term Transformation occurs?

---

ANALYSIS RULES

1. Base every answer primarily on the uploaded document.

2. Prefer explicit information over inferred information.

3. Infer only when the document strongly supports the conclusion.

4. Never invent missing information.

5. If a level cannot be confidently identified, write:

   "Not explicitly stated"

   and briefly explain why.

6. If the document contains multiple independent Ideas, analyse the primary one first. If appropriate, list additional Ideas separately.

7. For every answer, provide a brief justification based on the document.

8. If the reasoning chain contains logical gaps or inconsistencies, identify them.

9. If an Idea or Concept appears to rest on a false or unsupported Principle, explicitly point this out.
