The core distinction

You can think of the five formats as one content funnel:

Pin → Facebook Post → Article/Google Slide → Blog Post → Application

Or, more precisely:

  • Pin: “This problem exists—and there may be a better way.”
  • Facebook Post: “You should pay attention to this problem and this new idea.”
  • Article: “Let me help you understand the idea and why it matters.”
  • Google Slide: “Let me show you the idea visually and simply.”
  • Blog Post: “Now let me teach you exactly how to do it.”

The most important rule is therefore:

Do not make every content format a shortened copy of the Blog Post. Each format should perform a different job in moving the reader toward the Blog Post and, ultimately, toward application.
