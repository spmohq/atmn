Prompt #1: Transformation Extractor
Analyze the uploaded content and identify the primary transformation it promises, teaches, enables, or helps the reader achieve. Your task is to extract the transformation from the actual uploaded content, not to invent or improve it. Extract These Three Elements From: Identify the reader's starting situation, problem, limitation, struggle, or current state. To: Identify the reader's desired result, outcome, improvement, capability, or transformed state. Using: Identify the specific tool, process, method, framework, system, strategy, technique, resource, or mechanism the content uses to help the reader move from From to To. Extraction Rules - Base the extraction strictly on the uploaded content. - Identify the main or central transformation, not minor benefits or secondary outcomes. - Focus on the transformation experienced by the reader/user, not the features of the content itself. - The From and To statements must represent two meaningfully different states. - The To statement should describe a concrete outcome or benefit whenever the content supports one. - For Using, identify the actual mechanism taught or provided by the content. - Do not invent results, benefits, tools, numbers, timeframes, or claims that are not supported by the uploaded content. - Do not confuse an activity with a transformation. For example, “create 10 Pinterest pins” is an activity, while “generate consistent Pinterest traffic” may be the resulting transformation. - If the content contains multiple transformations, identify the single most important transformation that represents the core promise of the content. - Preserve the meaning of the source content rather than rewriting it into promotional language. - Do not create a headline or sales copy at this stage. Output Format From: [Starting situation, problem, limitation, or current state] To: [Desired result, outcome, improvement, or transformed state] Using: [Tool, process, method, framework, system, strategy, or mechanism] Final Instruction Return only the three extracted fields in the exact format above. Do not provide explanations, analysis, alternatives, or commentary.
