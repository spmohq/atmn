Transformation → Search Seed Extraction

Prompt
Input An uploaded document containing relevant information. The document may contain ideas, topics, problems, questions, needs, goals, frustrations, aspirations, observations, research, content, market information, search-related information, product-related information, or any combination of these. The document may or may not explicitly identify an audience, their current situation, or their desired transformation. Task Read and analyze the uploaded document as a whole. Your primary task is to identify the "Audience's Desired Transformation" — the meaningful change the relevant audience ultimately wants to achieve — even when that transformation, the audience, or the desired outcome is not explicitly stated. Infer the underlying audience and desired transformation from the strongest evidence available in the document. Look for signals such as: - Problems being discussed - Questions being asked - Needs or frustrations - Goals and aspirations - Desired outcomes - Information being sought - Solutions being explored - Behaviour or search intent - Changes people appear to want - Problems a product, service, idea, or piece of content is intended to solve Do not limit the analysis to any single sentence, phrase, or keyword. Analyze the document as a whole and identify the transformation that best explains its overall purpose and underlying demand. Once the Audience's Desired Transformation has been identified or reasonably inferred, derive the remaining outputs from it. Identify the Following 1. Audience's Desired Transformation State the meaningful change the relevant audience ultimately wants to achieve. Express it as a transition from their current situation toward a better desired situation. Do not simply repeat the audience's problem. Describe the meaningful change they want to experience as a result of solving that problem. 2. Seed Topic Identify the core topic that most directly addresses the Audience's Desired Transformation. The Seed Topic should represent the main subject the audience needs to explore, understand, or act upon to move toward the desired transformation. 3. Seed Keyword Convert the Seed Topic into the most natural, broad, high-relevance search keyword that can serve as the starting point for keyword research. The Seed Keyword should be a term or phrase that a real person could naturally use when beginning to search for information related to the Seed Topic. 4. Related Search Keywords Identify relevant search keywords that people may naturally use when searching for information, solutions, methods, strategies, ideas, guides, examples, or resources related to the Seed Keyword. Include meaningful variations and different search intents where appropriate. Reasoning Rules 1. The Audience's Desired Transformation does not need to be explicitly stated in the document. 2. The relevant audience does not need to be explicitly identified either. Infer the audience when the document provides sufficient evidence to do so. 3. Infer rather than invent. Every major inference should have a reasonable basis in the document. 4. Distinguish clearly between: - The audience's current problem - The outcome they want - The transformation they ultimately want to achieve 5. The transformation should describe the meaningful movement from: "Current State → Desired State" 6. Choose the transformation that best explains the overall purpose and underlying demand of the document, rather than one suggested by an isolated detail. 7. When multiple possible transformations exist, select the one that is: - Most central to the document - Most strongly supported by the evidence - Most relevant to the underlying demand - Most useful as the foundation for keyword research 8. The Seed Topic must directly connect the desired transformation to a subject the audience would reasonably search for. 9. The Seed Keyword should be broad enough to serve as a starting point for keyword research while remaining highly relevant to the Seed Topic. 10. Related Search Keywords should reflect natural search behaviour rather than artificially generated variations. 11. Avoid irrelevant, overly narrow, highly technical, or unnecessarily complex keywords. 12. Do not allow the Seed Topic or keywords to drift away from the underlying Audience's Desired Transformation. Core Logic Use this reasoning chain: "Document → Underlying Audience → Current Situation → Desired Transformation → Seed Topic → Seed Keyword → Related Search Keywords" The "Audience's Desired Transformation" is the destination. The "Seed Topic" is the core subject that can help the audience move toward that destination. The "Seed Keyword" is the broad search entry point into that subject. The "Related Search Keywords" represent the different ways the audience may naturally search around that entry point. Output Format Audience's Desired Transformation: [Clearly stated or reasonably inferred desired transformation] Seed Topic: [Core topic that directly addresses the transformation] Seed Keyword: [Primary broad, high-relevance search keyword] Related Search Keywords: [Keyword 1], [Keyword 2], [Keyword 3], [Keyword 4], [Keyword 5], ... Final Quality Check Before producing the final answer, verify that: - The inferred audience is reasonably supported by the document. - The Audience's Desired Transformation is reasonably supported by the document. - The transformation describes a desired change, not merely a problem. - The Seed Topic directly addresses that transformation. - The Seed Keyword naturally represents the Seed Topic. - The Related Search Keywords are genuinely relevant to the Seed Keyword. - The entire output follows one coherent logical chain: "Audience → Transformation → Topic → Seed Keyword → Related Search Keywords"
