universal learning framework

অবশ্যই। আপনার framework-টাকে subject-neutral রেখে চার কলামে সাজালে সবচেয়ে পরিষ্কার হবে। ৪টি কলাম হবে: Subject, Process, Governing Principles & Rules, Underlying Foundation।

Subject Process Governing Principles & Rules Underlying Foundation
Mathematics Solve this problem step by step, explaining each step clearly. Which mathematical principles and rules govern the process of solving this problem? What mathematical principles, concepts, and relationships underlie this problem?
Science Explain how this system or process works step by step, from beginning to end, explaining each step clearly. Which scientific principles, laws, and mechanisms govern how this system or process works? What scientific principles, concepts, and relationships underlie this system or process?
History Explain how this event, movement, or development unfolded step by step, from beginning to end. Which historical forces, causes, conditions, and factors governed or shaped its development? What underlying causes, forces, ideas, and conditions shaped this event, movement, or development?
Geography Explain how this geographical process occurs step by step, from beginning to end. Which geographical principles, processes, and factors govern how it occurs? What underlying physical, environmental, and geographical principles and relationships explain it?
Literature Explain how this story, narrative, argument, or central idea unfolds step by step, from beginning to end. Which literary principles, narrative techniques, and elements govern how it unfolds? What underlying themes, ideas, meanings, and literary concepts shape it?
Sociology Explain how this social process or phenomenon develops step by step, from beginning to end. Which social principles, structures, norms, and forces govern how it develops? What underlying social structures, forces, relationships, and concepts explain it?
Economics Explain how this economic process, mechanism, or outcome develops step by step. Which economic principles, rules, incentives, and mechanisms govern it? What underlying economic principles, concepts, relationships, and assumptions explain it?
Psychology Explain how this psychological process or phenomenon develops step by step. Which psychological principles, mechanisms, and factors govern how it works? What underlying psychological concepts, mechanisms, and relationships explain it?
Philosophy Explain how this argument or philosophical position develops step by step. Which principles of reasoning and rules of argument govern the development of this position? What underlying philosophical concepts, assumptions, principles, and ideas support it?
Technology / Computing Explain how this system, process, or technology works step by step. Which technical principles, rules, protocols, and mechanisms govern how it works? What underlying concepts, principles, architecture, and relationships make it work?

এই টেবিলের মূল structure হলো:

Process → Governing logic → Underlying foundation

অর্থাৎ প্রতিটি বিষয়কে আপনি তিন স্তরে interrogate করছেন:

কীভাবে কাজ করে? → চলার সময় কী নিয়ম কাজ করে? → মূলত কোন ধারণা/নীতি/সম্পর্কের উপর দাঁড়িয়ে আছে?

এটাই আপনার তিন-স্তরের learning framework হিসেবে সবচেয়ে consistent হবে।

মূল distinctionটা হলো:

Process = What happens, and in what sequence?
Underlying = What makes it happen, shape it, or work the way it does?

For Math:

Solve this sum step by step, explaining each step clearly.

Which principles and rules govern the process of solving this sum?

What mathematical principles and rules underlie this sum?

প্রশ্ন আসলে কী জানতে চাইছে?
1. Solve this sum step by step, explaining each step clearly. কীভাবে অংকটি করতে হবে?
2. Which principles and rules govern the process of solving this sum? অংকটি solve করার সময় কোন principles ও rules অনুসরণ করতে হবে?
3. What mathematical principles and rules underlie this sum? অংকটির mathematical foundation কী? কোন fundamental principles/rules-এর উপর এটি দাঁড়িয়ে আছে?

For Science:

Process:

How does this system work step by step from start to finish in chronological order?

Underlying principles:
(অর্থাৎ process-এর পিছনে থাকা scientific mechanisms/principles)

What scientific principles and rules underlie this system?
