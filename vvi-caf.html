Such a library would be a genuine reference system that you could:

  • consult when planning content,
  • use to train AI to choose the most effective framing,
  • integrate into your content workflow,
  • expand over time without changing the underlying structure.

I think this would become one of the most useful assets in your entire content system because it separates **what you want to say (the message)** from **how you choose to frame it (the angle)**—a distinction that many content frameworks overlook.
